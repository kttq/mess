# Nothing carries over at close

Close carries nothing over to the next month. An unpaid bill or an undone chore stays that way in its closed month, and the next month gets only the rows of the bills and chores that show in it. We chose this over a carried row for each open item because mess is a planner, not a tracker. Each month is a plan, and a closed month is the record of what happened. Carry-over would make mess track what the user owes.

## Consequences

- mess does not know about debts. If the user wants an old item in the open month, they add a one-off bill or chore (start month = end month) by hand. It has no link to the old row.
- A month row belongs to one month and one bill or chore. Do not add links from one month's row to another month's row.
- The close preview is the last warning about unpaid and undone rows. It does not stop close.
